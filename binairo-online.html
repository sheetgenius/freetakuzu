Binairo is the same logic puzzle many players also call Takuzu, binary puzzle, 0hh1, or Tic-Tac-Logic. Fill every cell with a 0 or a 1, obey three rules, and solve by deduction alone.

Play Binairo free

What makes Binairo work

  • No row or column can contain three identical digits in a row.
  • Every row and column must contain equal numbers of 0s and 1s.
  • No two rows or two columns can be identical.
