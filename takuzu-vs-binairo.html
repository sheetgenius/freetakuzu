Takuzu vs Binairo: the same binary puzzle

Takuzu and Binairo usually mean the same puzzle: a square grid filled with 0s and 1s under three constraints. The names come from different publishers and regions, not from different rules.

Common names

  • Takuzu - common generic name for the puzzle.
  • Binairo - widely used in Europe, especially for magazine-style binary puzzles.
  • Binary Puzzle - descriptive name based on the 0 and 1 grid.
  • 0hh1 and Tic-Tac-Logic - app and publisher names for the same puzzle family.

The rules are what matter

Whatever name you use, the solve is the same: avoid three identical digits in a row, balance every row and column, and prevent duplicate rows and columns.
